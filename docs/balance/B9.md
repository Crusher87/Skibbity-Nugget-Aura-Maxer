# B9: Pack and Kek economy

**Type:**
- Approved decisions D4 (Field 10% → 5%) and D5 (dupe Binder/Field → Kek).
- The Success Kid odds fix.
- Two economy options that **need Curtis's decision** (AUDIT §5.1, §5.2).

It runs last because B1–B8 change Kek income.

**Prerequisite:** B2 (`buyPack` adds the card and `awardKek` exists). Move `pullCard`'s literals into `BAL`, so `tools/card-sim.js` reads them from `BAL` instead of parsing the function body.

**Numbers:**
- Per-card odds are analytic.
- Set completion comes from a 2,000-trial Monte Carlo of `pullCard` (59 cards).
- Kek-to-complete is cumulative pack cost plus the 50 shop unlock.
- Kek income is 13.8/min at 6 cps.

### Implementation notes
- `BAL.PACK_FIELD_CHANCE 0.05`, `PACK_COMMON_CUT 0.60`, `PACK_RARE_CUT 0.88`, `PACK_PITY_DUPES 4`, `DUPE_KEK`, `KEK_PACK_COST_CAP 30`; `PACK_LUCK_MYTHIC_BONUS` default 0.02 → 0.01 to match Success Kid's new `mythicBonus: 0.01`.
- `pullCard` reads the cuts from `BAL`; Success Kid's `rareBonus` comes out of Common (`commonCut = PACK_COMMON_CUT − rareBonus`). New helpers: `packGroup`, `PACK_POOLS`, `packOdds(bs)` and `pullMissingCard()` (a not-owned card, each weighted by its normal per-pack odds).
- `buyPack`: pity swap when the pull is owned and `G.dupeStreak ≥ 4`; then `dupeStreak = isNew ? 0 : +1` (saved / loaded). A duplicate Binder/Field pays `DUPE_KEK[rarity]` at purchase (so a reload can't lose it); the reveal hint reads "Duplicate → +N 👑 · Click anywhere to collect" and the log line says it too.
- `getKekPackCost`: `min(KEK_PACK_COST_CAP, BASE + floor(n × SCALE))`, then the discounts and the `max(1, …)` floor as before.
- **Frenzy on passive APS (optional row): not implemented**, per Curtis (frenzy stays as it is).
- Sim: reads `BAL.PACK_*` (the `pullCard` text parser is gone), SK from Common, `packCost` cap, `buyPackSim` mirrors pity + D5 refund, and `completion()` reports net Kek (prices − refunds + shop) per trial. The Kek table gains "Minutes of clicking for this pack" and the longest single-pack wait.
